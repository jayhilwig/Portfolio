01 Overview

Amazon / Buyer Risk Prevention

Account recovery should take minutes, not days.

I designed two connected experiences;

  1. 1A clear way for customers to report unauthorized account activity.
  2. 2Guided workflows in an internal tool that help fraud investigators make informed decisions by following standard operating procedures (SOPs).

02 Problem & constraints

Help the right customer.
Stop the wrong activity.

A restricted account is a high-stakes moment. Legitimate customers need a way back in, while the recovery process has to surface useful evidence and support careful fraud decisions. The experience crosses two audiences with different needs.

Constraint / 01

Clarity for customers

Make it easy to identify suspicious activity without asking customers to understand internal risk processes.

Constraint / 02

Structured case context

Capture the relevant orders and details so the next person in the process can act on the report.

Constraint / 03

Judgment stays human

Support consistent, case-specific checks while keeping context and the final decision in the investigator’s hands.

03 Key design decisions

  1. Decision 01 / Customer reporting

    Start with what people recognize.

    The reporting experience asked customers to identify activity they did not authorize using terms they would recognize.

    Design rationale Make the first step concrete so the customer can provide usable input.

  2. Decision 02 / Order context

    Ask about the orders that matter.

    Customers could review suspicious orders and identify related activity before submitting a report.

    Design rationale Gather structured details while the customer still has the context to recognize them.

  3. Decision 03 / Investigator decision support

    Guide the decision. Keep the judgment.

    I designed dynamic, SOP-informed workflows that brought case-specific questions and customer information into a structured review experience for fraud investigators.

    Design rationale Support faster, more consistent work while keeping the final decision with the investigator.

04 Outcomes & evidence

Faster recovery. Better-supported decisions.
