Amazon / Buyer Risk Prevention

Designing recovery across customer and risk systems.

I designed two connected workflows inside Amazon Buyer Risk Prevention: a self-service recovery experience for customers and guided decision support for fraud investigators.

Customer self-service recovery

Helping legitimate customers recover access.

I designed a structured customer recovery flow that helped people report suspicious activity, review affected orders, and provide the information needed to unlock their account.

Guided workflows for investigators

Guide the decision without removing judgment.

I designed dynamic, SOP-informed workflows that helped BRP investigators review customer data, answer case-specific questions, and make faster, more consistent decisions while keeping control of the final judgment.
